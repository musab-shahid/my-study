## 5.8.1 PCA and EOF analysis

### 5.8.1.1 Data matrix construction
Common climate convention: **rows = time**, **columns = space** (stations or grid cells). Alternatives exist—**document your choice**.

**Centring:** remove the temporal mean at each location (often the climatology). Scaling (correlation-matrix PCA) changes relative importance of variables with different units—justify.

### 5.8.1.2 Area weighting
Equal-area treatment of lat–lon grids requires weights. A common approach multiplies each column by \(\sqrt{\cos\phi}\) (or equivalent) so polar cells do not dominate variance. For irregular station networks, weighting is a design choice (by area of influence, none, etc.)—state it.

### 5.8.1.3 Eigenvalue spectrum and SVD
Let \(\mathbf{X}\) be centred (and weighted) data. SVD \(\mathbf{X}=\mathbf{U}\boldsymbol{\Sigma}\mathbf{V}^\top\) yields EOFs (spatial patterns) and principal component time series. Eigenvalues of the covariance relate to squared singular values.

**North et al. rule of thumb:** eigenvalues that lie close within sampling error may yield mixed, unstable modes. Check split-period or bootstrap stability.

### 5.8.1.4 Sign ambiguity and interpretation
EOF signs are arbitrary (flip pattern and PC together). EOF1 of seasonal rainfall anomalies may *resemble* a monsoon variability pattern; it remains a **variance decomposition**, not proof of a teleconnection mechanism. Link to dynamics with separate physical evidence.

**Illustrative workflow (document your convention)**

Area weighting is applied so that the **covariance** reflects area, not grid-cell count. One common teaching approach:

1. Let \(w_j = \sqrt{a_j}\) with \(a_j \propto \cos\phi_j\) on a regular lat–lon grid.  
2. Form \(X^{w}_{tj} = X_{tj} w_j\) (columns scaled).  
3. SVD \(X^{w} = U\Sigma V^	op\). The right singular vectors live in **weighted** space.  
4. For a map in physical grid units, \(e_j = V_{1j}/w_j\) is a common display transform — but **reconstruction** must use a consistent inverse of the weighting convention you chose.  
5. Prefer validating on a synthetic field with a known spatial mode (see `fixtures/smoke_check.py`) before trusting signs/amplitudes on real data.

```python
import numpy as np

# X: (n_time, n_space), centred; w: sqrt(area) weights, length n_space
Xw = X * w
U, s, Vt = np.linalg.svd(Xw, full_matrices=False)
eof1_weighted = Vt[0, :]
eof1_map = eof1_weighted / w   # display on the physical grid (convention-dependent)
pc1 = U[:, 0] * s[0]
var_frac = s**2 / np.sum(s**2)
```
*Illustrative and unexecuted against real NetCDF in-repo. Run the synthetic smoke check to verify recovery of a known mode.*

## 5.8.2 MCA and CCA (overview)

Maximum covariance analysis and canonical correlation analysis find coupled patterns between two fields (e.g. SST and rainfall). They require careful standardisation, significance testing, and out-of-sample checks—easy to overfit on short climate records. Treat as advanced optional depth unless a project demands them.

---

## 5.8.3 Clustering and regimes

K-means, hierarchical clustering, and self-organising maps summarise recurring states (weather regimes, spectral clusters). Validate **stability** under resampling. For land-cover mapping, unsupervised clusters are not a substitute for supervised accuracy assessment with ground truth (Unit 4.7).

---

## 5.8.4 Composites, regression maps, indices, field significance

### 5.8.4.1 Indices
Standardise on a fixed reference period; document sign convention (e.g. positive = wetter). Example: regional mean PC1, or a standardized precipitation index at a chosen scale (Unit 2.6).

### 5.8.4.2 Composites and regression maps
Mean field conditional on an index threshold (e.g. high-PC1 years). Show sample size and uncertainty. Regression maps of a field on an index are associational.

### 5.8.4.3 Field significance
Testing many grid cells inflates false discoveries. Control false-discovery rate or use field-significance procedures (Wilks). Local “p < 0.05” stippling alone is not enough.

## Failure modes
- No area weighting on lat–lon grids when variance is spatial.  
- Retaining 20 modes because cumulative variance is 99% without stability checks.  
- Calling EOF1 “the climate-change signal.”  
- Composite maps without sample size or multiplicity control.  
- Treating K-means land-cover clusters as validated classification.
