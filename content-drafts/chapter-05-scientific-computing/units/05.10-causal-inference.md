# Unit 5.10 — Causal inference for observational environmental data · Support

**Exit competency:** causal question, assumptions, design, falsifiers.

Topics: potential outcomes; DAGs; confounding vs colliders; matching/weighting; DiD; synthetic control; RDD; IV (strict assumptions); ITS; spatial confounding and interference; sensitivity; language ladder (association → causal under assumptions → policy).

**Example:** tree planting vs LST — socioeconomic and density confounding; pixel NDVI regressions rarely identify policy effects.

**Deliverable:** ≤3-page causal design brief.
