# Unit 5.1 — Mathematical and physical foundations · Core

**Outcome:** dimensional analysis; derivatives/integrals; vectors/matrices/eigen/SVD intuition; simple ODEs; energy/mass balance; probability foundations.

## Learning outcomes (L3)
1. Unit conversions and order-of-magnitude checks for environmental quantities.
2. Finite differences and gradient intuition for fields/series.
3. Matrix–vector products and eigen/SVD meaning for data matrices.
4. Stock–flux ODE and Euler vs library integrators conceptually.
5. Surface energy balance and Stefan–Boltzmann with T in kelvin.
6. Expectation, variance, Bayes rule, common distributions, SE vs dependence.

## 5.1.1 Quantities and dimensional analysis
SI coherence; precipitation as depth vs rate; radiative fluxes in W m⁻²; LST storage in K. Dimensional homogeneity. ERA5 precipitation often in metres — convert deliberately; read accumulation docs (Unit 2.5).

**Practice:** Landsat ST scale/offset decode (verify current USGS factors before operational use).

## 5.1.2 Calculus
Derivative as local rate; centred finite difference; partial derivatives and ∇T; integral as accumulation (trapezoid intuition).

## 5.1.3 Linear algebra
Design matrix shape (rows = samples); projection intuition for OLS; eigenvalues of covariance as variance along PCs; SVD of centred data for PCA/EOF (Unit 5.8). Prefer `numpy.linalg.svd` / `eigh`.

**Micro-example:** S=[[1,0.8],[0.8,1]] → λ≈1.8, 0.2; PC1 along shared variability.

## 5.1.4 ODEs and numerics
dS/dt = in − out; steady state checks; forward Euler bias; `solve_ivp` for real work; stability/truncation concepts.

## 5.1.5 Physics for environmental modelling
Mass/water balance (include irrigation in Indus plain thinking). Surface energy balance Rn = H + LE + G (state sign convention). Stefan–Boltzmann E=εσT⁴. Advection/diffusion as vocabulary for model docs.

## 5.1.6 Probability
Axioms; conditional probability and Bayes (imperfect sensors); E[X], Var, Cov; Normal, Poisson, Gamma uses; CLT and **effective sample size** under autocorrelation.

## Common failure modes
°C in T⁴; depth/rate mix; false precision; i.i.d. SE on daily climate series; EOF eigenvalue as physical law.

## Deliverable
Units/conventions sheet for UHI or ERA5 project + one balance calculation + one small eigen/SVD demo + one probability calculation + uncertainty paragraph.

## References
Hartmann; Strang; Wilks; USGS ST docs; ERA5 docs (verify current).
