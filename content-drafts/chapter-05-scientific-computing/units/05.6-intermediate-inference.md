## 5.6.1 Multiple regression and structure

### 5.6.1.1 Matrix form
\[\mathbf{y}=\mathbf{X}\boldsymbol{\beta}+\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}\]
\(\beta_j\): change in expected \(Y\) per unit \(X_j\) holding other *included* predictors fixed—not “controlling for the world.”

### 5.6.1.2 Categorical predictors and interactions
Land-cover indicators; season × NDVI interactions. Plot simple effects when interpreting interactions.

### 5.6.1.3 Collinearity
VIF / condition indices. Highly correlated spectral indices yield unstable coefficients. Prefer substantive selection over kitchen-sink models.

---

## 5.6.2 Uncertainty quantification

**Parameter intervals.** Model-based SEs assume the error model; correlated residuals → SEs too small.

**Prediction intervals.** Wider than mean CIs; answer where a new observation may fall.

**Bootstrap / simulation.** Case or residual resampling; **block** bootstrap for series; spatial blocks for maps (Unit 3.9).

**Uncertainty taxonomy.** Measurement, representativeness, structural (model form), parametric, scenario, internal variability—name which your interval includes.

---

## 5.6.3 Dependence

Positive autocorrelation reduces effective sample size → inflated t-statistics if ignored.

**Practical responses**
- Cluster-robust SE by station, scene, or region.
- Explicit AR errors / GLS when appropriate.
- Spatial lag/error models only with clear justification.
- **Design-based:** spatial or temporal holdouts for predictive claims (preferred).

**Heuristic effective \(n\)** (AR(1)-like): \(n_{\mathrm{eff}}\approx n(1-\rho)/(1+\rho)\). Report sensitivity to \(\rho\).

---

## 5.6.4 Model comparison and out-of-sample evaluation

### 5.6.4.1 Baselines
Mean-only, climatology, persistence, simple regression. Complex models must beat simple baselines under the **same** split.

### 5.6.4.2 Metrics
RMSE, MAE, bias; careful use of \(R^2\); probabilistic scores when relevant. Match metric to decision (tail errors vs average).

### 5.6.4.3 Cross-validation design
Random K-fold **leaks** neighbours in space/time. Prefer leave-location-out, spatial blocks, or rolling-origin for time. Document the split **before** tuning.

### 5.6.4.4 Calibration and failure cases
Residual maps; performance by subgroup (urban/rural; wet/dry years).

**Worked sketch**

import statsmodels.formula.api as smf
fit = smf.ols("lst_c ~ ndvi + built_frac + C(season)", data=df).fit()
