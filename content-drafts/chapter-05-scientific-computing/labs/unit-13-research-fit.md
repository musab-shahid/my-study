## 2. Build your own card (offline)

1. Write a research-interest statement grounded in **methods you can evidence** (e.g. heat-screening protocol, LST literacy, reproducibility).
2. Map skills → evidence → gap → priority using the capstone and prior subjects.
3. Complete the application checklist flags honestly (most will start as `false`).
4. Draft a supervisor-enquiry checklist: 3–5 papers to read, one specific question, one-page summary attachment plan.
5. For funding: list **official pages you will check**; never invent deadlines or eligibility.

## Acceptance criteria

- Interest statement is specific and evidence-linked.
- Skill map has at least one high-priority gap with a realistic bridge.
- Non-claims include: not an admission offer; not a funding award; not supervisor commitment.
- Capstone limitations remain visible (synthetic data, LST ≠ air temperature, etc.).
- No fabricated professor names, programme rankings, or guaranteed scholarships.
