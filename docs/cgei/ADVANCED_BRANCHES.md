# Optional advanced environmental branches

These are optional extensions selected by research question, target role, or a demonstrated knowledge gap. They are not a fourteenth core CGEI subject and are not part of the canonical subject registry.

Choose one branch at a time. Before committing to a course or software sequence, state the question it answers, prerequisites, available data, and validation approach. Each branch should end in a small documented case study with assumptions, provenance, method checks, limitations, and a decision about whether the specialist method was necessary.

## Branches

- **Air-quality science:** emissions, atmospheric transport, exposure, and dispersion modelling.
- **Hydrology and applied hydraulics:** catchments, rainfall–runoff, floods, and hydraulic processes.
- **Geophysical hazards:** earthquakes, seismic risk, and relevant Earth processes.
- **Advanced quantitative decision methods:** econometrics, decision analysis, sensitivity analysis, and causal inference.
- **Qualitative and mixed methods:** interviews, participatory research, and mixed-methods design.
- **Environmental law and governance:** legal literacy, compliance, and environmental governance.

## Scope boundary

These branches extend rather than replace climate science, GIS, Earth observation, statistics, research design, and uncertainty practice. Software familiarity alone is not evidence of specialist competence. A specialist model requires domain-specific validation and explicit limitations.
